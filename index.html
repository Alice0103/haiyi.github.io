FIG. 01 — The designerRMIT · Interior

Profile

Spaces that
tell stories

"Adaptive reuse becomes a way to bridge the past and present — while imagining future possibilities for public space."

My work explores interiors as narratives — spaces that tell stories through fragments, memory, and material reuse. Trained at RMIT, I approach design as both a critical and poetic practice. From speculative installations to adaptive reuse proposals, I seek to reveal how design can transform overlooked materials, forgotten sites, and everyday experiences into meaningful spatial encounters — working between Melbourne and Beijing.

Since stepping into professional practice, my focus has gradually shifted from spatial design alone toward an AI-driven, interdisciplinary way of working — helping companies deliver design tasks and visual experiences across graphic, spatial, product, and web design. Looking ahead, I hope to keep pushing the boundaries of AI tools, uncover new opportunities in the market, and shape the most efficient, highest-quality design workflows.
